# Beta Reading Package

Everything needed to run the multi-profile beta panel for *The Shape Love Takes*. Supports issue #26 and backlog TWI-305 (recruit readers) and TWI-306 (run questionnaire, triage feedback).

## Contents

- **`READING_COPY.md`** — the full manuscript compiled into one file (~67,180 words, Prologue + 29 chapters). Regenerate after any manuscript change by concatenating `manuscript/[0-9]*.md` in order.
- **`BETA_PANEL_PLAN.md`** — panel size and composition (5 to 7 readers, one per lens), content/sensitivity notes, logistics, and the triage method for handling feedback.
- **`BETA_READER_BRIEF.md`** — the cover note sent to each reader: what the book is, what to watch for, what's intentional, confidentiality, content heads-up, timeline.
- **`BETA_QUESTIONNAIRE.md`** — 30 questions tuned to this book's specific risks (does "recovery is the horror" land, is the illness lethal enough, does Brooke stay sympathetic, does the restrained ending work, does the grief land, where does it drag).

## How to run it

1. Recruit 5 to 7 readers across the lenses in the panel plan (TWI-305). Send each the brief first and confirm the content notes are okay for them.
2. Send the reading copy plus the questionnaire. Ask for margin marks (bored / confused / scared / moved) as they read.
3. Give them 3 to 4 weeks.
4. Collect, tag, and triage per the panel plan's method — weight by pattern, not volume; protect the load-bearing design choices unless the panel shows they are failing (TWI-306).
5. Produce a ranked revision list; make those changes before copyedit (issue #30).

## Before recruiting

Optional AI **dry-run panel**: simulate an in-character read from each lens and answer the questionnaire as that reader, to catch the cheap, obvious problems before real readers spend their attention. A rehearsal, not a replacement — it cannot tell you whether real people will care or feel harmed.
