# Beta Reader Brief

Thank you for reading *The Shape Love Takes*. Your honest reaction is worth more to me than a kind one. Please read the whole thing before answering the questionnaire, and don't worry about sparing my feelings — I can't fix what I don't hear.

## What it is

A psychological family-horror novel, about 67,000 words. Seventeen-year-old Brooke films everything. When an illness sweeps her town and her little sister Claire "recovers," Brooke becomes convinced that the people who came back are not quite the people who left — and that no one will believe her.

## What I need from you

- **Read like a reader, not an editor.** I care most about what you *felt* and where you *drifted*, not about typos.
- **Mark where you got bored or skimmed.** A quick "skimmed here" in the margin or a chapter number is gold.
- **Mark where you were confused** — not "intriguing mystery" confused, but "I don't know what just happened / I had to reread" confused.
- **Mark the moments that landed** — where you felt dread, grief, or the urge to keep reading.
- Then answer the **questionnaire** (`BETA_QUESTIONNAIRE.md`). Answer from memory and impression; don't study.

## A few things that are intentional (so you can tell me if they *failed*, not just that they're there)

I'm telling you these only so you can judge whether they work — please still flag them if they didn't:

- The cause of the illness is **never explained**. You are meant to be left with dread, not a diagnosis.
- Some threads are **left open** on purpose (one character's fate; what happens after the last page).
- The ending is deliberately **quiet and restrained** rather than explosive.

If any of those frustrated you rather than unsettled you, that's exactly what I need to know.

## Please don't

- Share, quote, post, or forward any part of this draft. It's an unpublished confidential copy.
- Look up or coordinate with the other readers before you finish. I want your first, uninfluenced reaction.

## Heads-up on content

The book includes a child's terminal illness and death (offstage), other deaths in the community, coercive family control, a cruel remark that weaponizes adoption and abandonment, medical trauma, and a gun fired inside a house during a break-in. No sexual content. If any of that is a hard limit for you, tell me now and we'll find a better fit — no hard feelings.

## Timeline

Take about **three to four weeks**. If life gets in the way, tell me; a late honest read beats a rushed one. When you're done, send the questionnaire back however is easiest.

Thank you. Truly.
