# Book One Closure Contract

**Status:** Locked by the author during Phase 6

*The Shape Love Takes* is explicitly positioned as **Book One**. This label is a publishing and structural promise, not permission to leave the current volume without an emotional resolution.

## Current-volume promises resolved

- Brooke stops waiting for family, institutions, or a perfect recording to validate what she has observed.
- She acts on her evidence, survives the bedroom siege, escapes with Cooper, and keeps the surviving record with her.
- She acknowledges Claire's truthful denial and apologizes for violating Claire's room, preserving the sister relationship beneath the horror without implying recovery.
- She tells Sarah that she is still her mother and that Brooke loves her, but rejects captivity as the definition of love.
- She leaves Sarah's final technically true plea unanswered.
- She tests Anna's practical boundaries and initiates the final embrace herself. Chosen closeness, rather than isolation alone, is Brooke's meaningful Book One transformation.

This resolution does not equal safety. The reader-only journal image immediately turns Brooke's new agency into the next threat without undoing the fact that she exercised it.

## Intentional continuation promises

- Anna's nature, prior history, surveillance, journal study, and future plans
- Mark's unknown fate in western Pennsylvania
- the illness's cause, route, intelligence, and wider trajectory
- the emergency red formulation's relationship, if any, to illness or change
- Claire and Sarah's retained personality, agency, and possible future actions
- Cooper's continuing unease
- the motel and Brooke's destination after the immediate escape
- delayed police response and the aftermath at the Bennett house

These are Book One continuation threads, not evidence that the current-volume emotional arc is unfinished.

## Ending restraint

- Keep Chapter 29's journal image and final sentence unchanged.
- Do not add mythology explanation, an extended coda, a sequel preview, or a false declaration of safety.
- Do not invent a series title, sequel title, planned volume count, or final destination without author approval.
- Optional adaptation and story-universe work is not a blocker for publishing Book One.

## Publishing position

- Adult psychological horror with strong upper-YA crossover appeal
- Book One, with no approved series name or announced volume count
- Content guidance should accurately account for graphic gastrointestinal illness, coercive family confinement, child endangerment, stalking/doxxing, institutional invalidation, and firearm violence
